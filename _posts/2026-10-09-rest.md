i woke up today, felt like my body is crumbling apart, and decided to skip class. this is one of the best decision i've made recently.

yesterday was a hefty day, alright? i got 2 classes very close to each other. i skipped lunch, i did not have enough nap. i squeeze every juice i have left in my brain to tell claude what to do next so it can write down in somewhat readable form and future me will know (not me right now). and today, i feel like i was hit by a truck *mentally*. i have no idea what i am during, no goal, just wandering around my room aimlessly.

i woke up, i ate breakfast. yesterday i was thinking something along this line: damn, i made a lot of decisions in one day. for example: i have a dynamic morning routine. when i wake up, i will make breakfast, boil the water to prepare for my coffee, then i brush my teeth in the mean time or i actually cook something to eat. to make something to eat, i have to decide what (from my limited choices of meal) to eat, then make it. when making it, i have a mental manual to follow through and not mess things up. after breakfast, i have my first sip of coffee, feel marginally better, and go hit the shower to actually wake me up. in the shower, again, a lot of things to do, following an order. i just subconsciously do it without notice, because i've been doing this the whole time.

it makes sense that you don't really think about it, but i do, so i think about it. in each step, no matter how small, i still have to make some sort of decisions accordingly. i would not want to have my coffee cold, and i would not want to overcook my eggs. it is small and mighty and all that jazz, but to me, when you group everything together, it can be quite a big mental effort i need to pull off.

somedays, i wake up, do all of this, and be tired already. somedays i have more to spare, so i work. most of the day i'm in the former situation, though.

i feel like i'm always running on tight mental budget. it is a weird thing to think about (is it?). but for many people, waking up and doing stuffs are things they can just ... do. but i can not without thinking about them. and it is exhausting. i do not want to deal with other bullshits, especially those the come up to me when i am **actually* focusing on finishing my work. i am always greatful for myself to have those times, hyperfocus type shii time, to work on important thing, for myself. it is not frequent, but it happens. but then some of the bullshit thing will get around and steer me from my work, and i hate that.

sometimes i want to tell people to shut up and leave me alone, but i am not a rude person so i just shut myself down and turn everything off in hope that if i can not see it, it will not affect me. and then, i drift away.

i put on some music. i have been listening to this one album "sweet fortune" for nearly 3 weeks now, on repeat, with little other music in between. it only happens to some albums that i can listen to them over and over and over again and still find them interesting and not get bored and find new music. and i love the heck out of this album.

as i put on some music, my mind continues to drift away. i think about the old time (good ol' time, they say), and wonder why am i here today, in this place, doing this? what am i supposed to do? am i actually living or just pretend to live and actually drowning inside.

i was accepted to a master program and let me tell you it is so batshit but due to my respect to other prof and people who work at the university, i am not complaining about them. it is just that i feel it is not *that* important? it ... i feel weird writing this down because it is contradicting on so many level and i just can not understand why. but it *is*

anyway, the thing is, i do not want anyone to disturb me anymore. i want to get the money and learn and do research! not participating in these outgoing weird shit that i have been going through. i have *no* need for that. i work better when there are few people who actually understand me and talk to me when i need and leave me alone most of the time.

think whatever you want, but i tried integrating myself into places for many time i just tired of doing so. so i stop trying and work on the thing that matters to me. it will not earn me a massive recognition but i feel like i am respecting myself when i work on things that align with me and not going around parading to people.

still being thankful for the environment though.

anyway, as the album going on, i look at the window, big large window in my room and see a clouded sky. today's a great day, not gonna lie. very suitable for lying down, doing nothing, and letting loose.

i heard one social media (instagram reels) that if you do not let yourself rest, then your body will force you to do so, by shutting itself down. funni. and i did experience some sort of that yesterday and today. yesterday was ... draning, and today, i wake up, feel like a million bucks, and skip class. i can not focus on anything and my mind is blank. yes, my body is shutting itself off so i can not do anything so it can rest and restore whatever kind of energy it needs. i used to think it is utter useless to let 1 day pass away without doing anything, but through many of days like this, i realize rest *is* part of the process. working continuously and i am putting myself to failure.

so, learned it the hard way. what's the next lesson? doing thing slowly is better than just let claude do everything and then not understanding one shit. maybe it is going on now.

still, cloudy day, clouded mind, confused movement and lifeless gestures. this is me today, thank you very much.

i love doing thse kind of thought dump. this is mentally relaxing. words vomit from my brain in no particular order. i feel like everything and nothing.

i **really* should sign off instagram.

that's it for now.
