yes, as the title direct, i am not good at communication. i hate that fact because i have no idea how to talk to others without feeling like a nuisance. shower thought mid-pre-blog but like i feel like i am claude with more agency. yes, i know that dividing a big, huge tasks into managable, bite size tasks is important when doing a project, either research or industry. but i just can not read people's mind. i feel bad enough sometimes for go radio-silent on people without telling them ahead and now i'm tasting my own medicine. it is restless enough.

i'm writing this while waiting for some important task from a significant figure to complete it, and i'm restless but i'm scared to ask again, because, as mentioned above, i do not know how to ask without sounding like nagging people or being a nuisance. this makes me want to disappear more than anything. part of me hates it, because i have to rely on someone higher-up and i do not know anything on the other side, aside from some vague instruction. i am just *terrible* at waiting and being patient, am i?

i'd rather being called there and ask for direct information and spill it out in one go, rather than waiting in vain like this. my anxiety is bad enough, thank you very much, and i do NOT need to keep it higher, or i might collapse. i can understand for people, to some certain extents, and i should not be angry, or ungrateful, but my body is acting against my will and i can not keep myself under control if being pushed to the very end. 

i am feeling nauseous, i might as well have to go for a check, one last time, to end it all. i just want this post to be a future reminder to not procrastinate, though i will, but don't be too late. because i know my nerves will not receive it well if i push myself too far on the other side. and for once, i do not want to have another seizure episode.

in short, please if anyone works with me, higher-ups, peers, or lowers, please just write it out for me, please. your feedback is indeed, very valuable for me, and to not make me restless like this again.

i should write more, but i couldn't care less, but hey writing is a great stress relief, one of my favourite coping mechanism.
