# Banlist

A seed list, not a closed set. The governing rule is the **deletion test** in `SKILL.md`: ban any word that claims a quality rather than showing it, whether or not it appears here. Extend by analogy. Categories are for organizing the list, not a limit on what is banned.

Context matters: a few of these words carry real signal in a specific technical claim (e.g. *significant* in a statistical result, *critical* as a graph-theory term). The ban targets the vague, quality-asserting use, not the precise one. When in doubt, apply the deletion test.

---

## A — Sincerity / stance markers

Claim candour or directness the prose should not need to announce. Almost always deletable.

honestly, honest, in all honesty, to be honest, if I am honest, the honest truth, in truth, truly, genuinely, really (as intensifier), frankly, candidly, to be fair, to be clear, let me be clear, make no mistake, admittedly (when hollow), needless to say, it goes without saying.

Watch especially for **"honest caveat"** and **"one honest caveat"** — flag on sight.

## B — Self-importance adjectives

Assert weight, depth, or importance instead of demonstrating it. Replace with the fact that shows it, or delete.

load-bearing, crucial, vital, key, essential, critical, pivotal, paramount, significant, profound, deep, powerful, robust, seamless, meaningful, fundamental, invaluable, indispensable, remarkable, notable, compelling, striking, game-changing, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, unparalleled, unprecedented, transformative, revolutionary.

**Intensifiers** (same family, delete by default): very, extremely, incredibly, remarkably, notably, particularly, highly, deeply, truly, quite, utterly, absolutely, immensely, tremendously.

## C — Cliche connectives and transitions

Filler joins and framing phrases that signal AI cadence. Cut or replace with a plain connective.

moreover, furthermore, additionally, in addition, that said, having said that, with that said, it is worth noting, it's worth noting, it is important to note, importantly, notably, crucially, ultimately, in conclusion, to sum up, in summary, all in all, at the end of the day, when it comes to, in terms of, as such, first and foremost, on the one hand / on the other hand (as a reflexive pair), in today's world, in the modern era, in an increasingly connected world, now more than ever, at its core, at its heart.

## D — Cliche framing verbs and nouns

The "delve" family: metaphor-as-jargon and inflated vocabulary that reads as machine-generated.

**Verbs:** delve, delve into, leverage, utilize, underscore, showcase, foster, cultivate, harness, unlock, unpack, navigate (metaphorical), embark, embark on a journey, dive in, deep dive, boast / boasts, spearhead, elevate, empower, streamline.

**Nouns:** tapestry, landscape, realm, sphere, arena, testament, journey, ecosystem, paradigm, synergy, myriad, plethora, wealth of, treasure trove, beacon, cornerstone, backbone, bedrock, spectrum, lens (as in "through the lens of"), interplay.

**Adjectives (metaphorical / vague):** holistic, nuanced (as filler), multifaceted, intricate, vibrant, rich (metaphorical), dynamic, comprehensive (as filler), versatile.
